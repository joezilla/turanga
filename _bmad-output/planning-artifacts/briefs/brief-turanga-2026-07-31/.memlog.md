- (event) Competitive research 2/4 (Openclaw+Hermes) complete: BOTH confirmed as agent runtimes and NEITHER isolation-first by default — validates user's complaint. OpenClaw (github.com/openclaw/openclaw): sandbox opt-in (mode OFF by default) but hardened Docker defaults once on (network:none, ro root, capDrop ALL); tool-policy scoping; no injection defense, no cost limits; public sandbox-escape write-ups (Snyk/Lasso). Neither has spend caps. Caveat: 'Hermes' also = Nous LLM family (distinct).
